1The 30-second read

Launched 28 September. 11 seats so far. The page converts; the price of a Facebook view is the problem, and it is coming down.

  • We launched on Monday 28 September. Seven days later we have 11 paid seats from the campaign on $5,758 of spend (92% of the week-one plan budget), 3 of them this morning.
  • The first four days were a testing phase: formats, audiences, cost caps and placements. $2,671 bought 2 seats and the answers we needed.
  • The last three days were the selling phase: $3,087 bought 9 seats. Cost per seat went $1,336 → $343 → $242 (testing → selling → since Sunday noon).
  • The page works: 2.2% of visitors buy (plan 1.5%). The problem is the price of the visitor: Facebook charges us $241–344 per 1,000 views against a plan of $20. Every change we made attacks that number, and creator videos already bring visitors at a third of the film's cost.
  • Plan to the event stays inside your budget. Base forecast 270–290 seats by 27 Oct, with cost per seat falling into the $90 range by week 4. Budget follows cost per seat: we scale what sells under target and cut what does not. The gates on slide 13 decide scale or stop.
