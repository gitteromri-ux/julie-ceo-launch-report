What happened, in order

  • 28 Sep – 2 Oct, launch. Two ad sets, 45–64 interest audiences, static banners plus Julie's 65-second film. CPM $140–420. 4 sales, $830 each. Diagnosis: reach is the constraint — the audience Meta could find at that targeting cost 20–30× the Facebook-feed market, and the landing page converted 2.4% of visitors to checkout either way.
  • 3 Oct, restructure. 64 ads switched off, 8 removed, 27 new ads created (presenter UGC videos and 9:16 Reels videos). Six ad sets: 03 and 04 widened to 35–65. Placements cut to Facebook feed, Facebook Reels and Instagram feed. The Reels-overlay placement bought 28,997 impressions at $9.6 CPM that day — 193 visits, 1 checkout open, 0 sales — and was removed the same evening: cheap reach without intent does not convert.
  • 4 – 6 Oct, consolidation. Budget moved into the two sets that open checkouts: set 04 (UGC) $150 → $579/day, set 03 (Julie film) $199 → $459/day. Sets 01, 06 cut; Blueprint cut to $103/day. Inside set 04 the two non-selling UGC videos were paused; only the two that sell run. Result: 9 sales in 2.5 days, today at $266 per sale.
